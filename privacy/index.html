Privacy

This page covers the syntheo.studio site. The privacy of the Syntheo application itself is described in the documentation: Data and privacy and Syntheo Desktop and the GDPR.

What the site does not do

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics or audience measurement.
  • No third-party fonts, scripts or other resources: everything the pages load comes from syntheo.studio.

What your browser keeps

Two choices are stored in your browser (localStorage), never sent to the site:

  • the language, when you pick EN or FR (syntheo-lang, assets/js/lang.js);
  • the theme, when you pick light or dark (syntheo-theme, assets/js/theme.js).

You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The host

GitHub, which hosts the site, logs the IP address of visitors in its technical logs, for security purposes (GitHub Pages documentation). See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The download links and the links to the source code lead to github.com, which then also sees your IP address.
